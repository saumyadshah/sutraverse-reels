The idea: Chanakya's 3 signs of a home Lakshmi enters on her own. Chanakya Niti 3.21 (Niti-darpana numbering; varies by edition): "Where fools aren't honoured, where grain is well stored, where husband and wife don't quarrel, there Shri (Lakshmi) comes of her own accord."
मूर्खा यत्र न पूज्यन्ते धान्यं यत्र सुसञ्चितम् । दाम्पत्ये कलहो नास्ति तत्र श्रीः स्वयमागता ॥
Notice: not one of the three is about cleaning.
