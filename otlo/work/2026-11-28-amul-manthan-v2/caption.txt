This film's producer wasn't a studio. It was 5,00,000 Gujarati farmers 🎬
આ ફિલ્મના પ્રોડ્યુસર? પાંચ લાખ ગુજરાતી ખેડૂતો, દરેકના ₹2.

Manthan (1976) tells the story of Gujarat's milk cooperative movement, inspired by Dr Verghese Kurien and Amul in Anand. To make it, Shyam Benegal had a budget of about ₹10 lakh, and the dairy farmers of the Gujarat Co-operative Milk Marketing Federation paid for it: ₹2 each.
That's why the film opens with "500,000 farmers of Gujarat present". When it released, farmers came by the truckload to watch "their" film. In 2024, the Film Heritage Foundation's 4K restoration had its world premiere in Cannes Classics.
