02
ઘરે શું થાય?
What many families do
An early holy dip in a river, diyas lit at dusk, and worship of Shiva and Vishnu. It is also called Tripurari Purnima. Why? That's tomorrow's Reel.
સવારે સ્નાન, સાંજે દીવા, શિવ-વિષ્ણુની પૂજા.
03
સોમનાથ
Somnath: a fair by the sea
The Kartik Purnima melo is held on the seashore around the full moon, run by the temple trust: darshan, folk programmes, stalls.
દરિયા કિનારે કાર્તિકી પૂનમનો મેળો.
04
વૌઠાનો મેળો
Vautha, near Dholka
Known as Saptasangam, a confluence of rivers where the Sabarmati and Vatrak meet. Pilgrims bathe at the sangam; the fair is famous for its donkey and camel market.
સંગમમાં સ્નાન, ગધેડાં-ઊંટનું બજાર.
05
શામળાજી
Shamlaji, on the Meshwo
Pilgrims bathe in the Meshwo river before darshan of Vishnu as Gadadhar. Bhil devotees call him Kaliyo Dev. The fair runs about three weeks around Kartik Purnima.
મેશ્વોમાં સ્નાન, પછી શામળિયાનાં દર્શન.
