Diwali's over? Not for the gods. Dev Diwali is tomorrow, and here's where Gujarat celebrates it.

Kartik Purnima falls on Tuesday 24 Nov 2026. The Purnima tithi runs from 11:42 PM on Monday to 8:23 PM on Tuesday, and lamps are lit in pradosh, 5:08–7:47 PM IST (Drik Panchang).
Many families take an early river dip, light diyas at dusk and worship Shiva and Vishnu. Somnath holds its Kartik Purnima melo by the sea, Vautha near Dholka is known for its sangam dip and donkey and camel market, and Shamlaji's fair on the Meshwo runs about three weeks.
