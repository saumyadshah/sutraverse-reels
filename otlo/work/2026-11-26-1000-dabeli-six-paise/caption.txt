Your dabeli once cost six paise. Here's where it started.

Mandvi, Kutch, the 1960s: Kesha Malam is said to have made the first dabeli and sold it for one anna (six paise).
The name is the recipe: dabeli means "pressed", masala pressed into pav. His family still runs the shop.
