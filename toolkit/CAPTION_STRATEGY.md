# SutraVerse Reel caption & Instagram SEO strategy (v4, 30 Oct 2026: real-life first + series + comment-to-DM)

> Also read toolkit/CONTENT_STANDARDS.md (hooks, story structure, shloka explanations, visuals, carousels, Stories). It overrides older instructions where they conflict.

Applies to every SutraVerse Reel AND carousel caption and its metadata (carousel differences in "Carousels" below). Goal: a recognisable SutraVerse identity plus relevant organic discovery, watch time, saves, shares, meaningful comments and follows. Relevance and readability beat volume: no keyword stuffing, no misleading clickbait, no irrelevant trending terms, no promises of virality.

## Caption structure (in this order)

1. **Hook line** (first ~125 characters, before Instagram's "…more"): the specific real-life moment from the Reel, written so the reader recognises their own life, with the search phrase inside it naturally. It must name a concrete situation, not a feeling.
   - Good: "Your manager presented YOUR slides to the CEO and didn't say your name. Karna knew that feeling. 💼"
   - Good: "Mummy: 'Sharma ji ka beta toh…' 🙃 Dhruva heard worse at age five, from his stepmother."
   - Bad: "Feeling unappreciated? 😔" (generic), "VIRAL!!! You won't BELIEVE this 🤯" (clickbait), or a list of keywords.
2. **Story / context** (1–2 short lines): what happened next in the modern scene, then the scripture parallel told simply, with the verse reference.
3. **Takeaway** (1 line): one meaningful, practical life lesson the viewer can use today.
4. **Engagement prompt** (conversational, 1–2 lines; vary it):
   - a comment question with a side to pick or a story to tell, tied to the exact situation ("Have you ever had your work stolen in a meeting? What did you do? 👇", "Team A: speak up in the meeting / Team B: talk to the boss after");
   - a share nudge that names a specific person ("Send this to the colleague who always says 'we' when it was you", "Tag the cousin who's always compared with you") and/or a save nudge for a real moment ("Save this for your next appraisal meeting");
   - a follow line, e.g. "Follow @sutraverselife for daily life lessons from ancient wisdom."
5. **Keyword line** (one line, after a blank line), 10–15 keywords in total, separated by " · ", no repeats, written in readable Title Case:
   - **Always** the three brand keywords: SutraVerse · Ancient Wisdom · Life Lessons
   - **4–7 situation keywords (the main lever since v3): the words people actually type about the problem**, chosen from the Reel's territory, e.g.
     - office: Office Politics · Corporate Life · Toxic Boss · Toxic Workplace · Workplace Problems · Work Life · Credit Stealing Colleague · Appraisal · Burnout · Job Stress · Career Advice · Manager Problems
     - family: Indian Family · Desi Parents · Log Kya Kahenge · Family Pressure · Sibling Rivalry · Marriage Pressure · Indian Mom · Joint Family · Parents Comparison
     - relationships/friends: Friendship · Fake Friends · Toxic Relationship · Relationship Advice · Self Respect · Moving On · Trust Issues · Jealousy
     - social/everyday: Social Pressure · Comparison · Overthinking · Setting Boundaries · Saying No · Money Lending · Adulting · Relatable
     Pick the ones that match the Reel's exact situation; never a list from another territory.
   - **0–3 broad discovery keywords**, only if room: Relatable Reels · Life Advice · Self Improvement · Mindset · Human Psychology · Practical Life Lessons. No "Viral Reel" / "Trending Reels" (they add nothing).
   - **1–3 scripture keywords for the parallel used** (it is our differentiator): e.g. Bhagavad Gita · Ramayana · Mahabharata · Upanishads · Vedas · Puranas · Chanakya Niti · Sanatan Dharma · Karma · Dharma · Spiritual Wisdom · the main character's name.
   - Pick the set by search intent: what would someone who should see this Reel type into Instagram search?
6. **Exactly 5 hashtags** (one line, after a blank line):
   - #SutraVerse (always first);
   - 2 situation tags (the real-life problem: e.g. #OfficePolitics #CorporateLife #DesiParents #IndianFamily #Friendship #ToxicWorkplace);
   - 1 scripture tag for the parallel (e.g. #Mahabharata, #BhagavadGita, #Ramayana);
   - 1 broad tag (e.g. #LifeLessons, #RelatableReels, #SelfImprovement).
   - Choose them from the pools in "Hashtag & keyword research" below. No banned, spammy or unrelated tags; never more or fewer than 5.

Length: total under 2,200 characters; aim for about 500–1,100. Short paragraphs, 1–3 tasteful emojis at most. English, with a Hinglish phrase only when it makes a line more relatable.

## Hashtag & keyword research (applies to every Reel)

Instagram enforces a maximum of 5 hashtags per post/Reel (since Dec 2025), counting caption and first comment together. So hashtags never go in the first comment, and the real discovery levers are the words Instagram indexes: the caption (especially line 1), the on-screen English text, and the spoken audio.

### 1. The search we're trying to win
Before writing anything, write down the ONE phrase a real person would type into Instagram search that this Reel should answer, e.g.:
"colleague took credit for my work", "toxic boss", "office politics", "how to deal with office politics", "parents comparing me with others", "log kya kahenge", "fake friends", "friend only calls when they need something", "marriage pressure", "how to say no to relatives", "burnout at work", "mahabharata lessons for office".
Pick by the Reel's real situation: the problem first, the scripture second ("office politics mahabharata" beats "mahabharata story"). Use hashtag-keyword-research to check the phrase people actually use (Hinglish phrasing is fine: "log kya kahenge").

### 2. Caption keyword (line 1)
The searchable phrase appears naturally in the first line or the first sentence, e.g. "The Bhagavad Gita's fix for overthinking at 2 a.m. 🪔". Never a bare keyword list as the hook.

### 3. Spoken keyword (in the narration)
Within the first 5 seconds of the Hindi voice-over, say the searchable phrase out loud, clearly, in natural Hinglish, e.g. "Office politics ka sabse purana example… Mahabharat mein hai.", "Jab manager aapka credit le le…". Say the character's and scripture's names clearly at least once more later. Transcripts are indexed.

### 4. On-screen keyword
The first on-screen English text and the cover title contain the topic words ("Gita's Fix for Overthinking"), and the subtitle line that carries the spoken keyword shows it in English.

### 5. Hashtags: CORE + ROTATING, exactly 5
- CORE (every Reel): #SutraVerse
- SITUATION (pick 2 that match the exact problem; this is where reach comes from):
  - Office: #OfficePolitics #CorporateLife #OfficeLife #ToxicBoss #ToxicWorkplace #WorkLife #CorporateHumour #JobLife
  - Family: #IndianFamily #DesiParents #DesiFamily #IndianParents #SiblingLove #JointFamily #LogKyaKahenge
  - Relationships / friends: #Friendship #FakeFriends #RelationshipAdvice #ToxicRelationship #SelfRespect #TrueFriends
  - Social / everyday: #Relatable #Overthinking #SocialPressure #Adulting #Boundaries #MiddleClassLife
- SCRIPTURE (pick 1 for the parallel): #Mahabharata #Mahabharat #BhagavadGita #Ramayana #Krishna #Karna #ChanakyaNiti #Upanishads
- BROAD (pick 1): #LifeLessons #RelatableReels #SelfImprovement #AncientWisdom #Mindset
- Festival tags (#KarwaChauth, #Diwali…) only on the festival's Reel, replacing the broad tag.
- Order: #SutraVerse, situation tags, scripture tag, broad tag. Rotate within each pool; never the same 5 two days running.

### 6. AVOID
- Generic, low-intent or engagement-bait tags: #fyp #viral #explorepage #explore #reels #reelsinstagram #trending #instagood #follow4follow #like4like #foryou.
- Off-topic trending tags, tags about other creators/brands, misspelled or banned tags, and political or communal tags.
- More or fewer than 5 hashtags; hashtags in the first comment.

## Pinned first comment
One easy, specific question that invites a real answer (not "thoughts?"). Different from the caption's question.

## On-screen and in-video SEO
- The cover title and the first on-screen English text state the topic clearly in plain words (e.g. "Krishna's 1 Rule for Overthinking"), so Instagram and viewers understand it.
- Subtitles stay English; key terms (the character, scripture or the life lesson) appear on screen at least once.

## Carousels (same strategy, adapted)
Everything above applies to carousel posts too, with these differences:
- No narration, so there is no spoken keyword. Instead the COVER slide title carries the search phrase's topic words in plain English (e.g. "Gita's Fix for Overthinking"), and the topic words appear again on at least one inside slide.
- Caption line 1 contains the search phrase naturally; the takeaway can point to the slides ("Slide 4 is the one to screenshot").
- Engagement prompt leans on SAVE and SHARE (carousels are saved and sent more than Reels), plus one easy comment question and the follow line.
- Same keyword line (always starting "SutraVerse · Ancient Wisdom · Life Lessons") and exactly 5 hashtags from the same pools; no hashtags in the first comment.
- Add alt text per slide when the tool allows it (plain description + the topic words).

## Tone
Warm, observant, a little witty, like a friend who has seen the same office and family drama; culturally respectful; never preachy. The modern scene can be funny; the scripture is always handled with respect.
