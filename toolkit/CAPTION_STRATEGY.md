# SutraVerse Reel caption & Instagram SEO strategy

Applies to every SutraVerse Reel caption and its metadata. Goal: a recognisable SutraVerse identity plus relevant organic discovery, watch time, saves, shares, meaningful comments and follows. Relevance and readability beat volume: no keyword stuffing, no misleading clickbait, no irrelevant trending terms, no promises of virality.

## Caption structure (in this order)

1. **Hook line** (first ~125 characters, before Instagram's "…more"): instant interest from a relatable modern moment or a surprising story beat. Name the topic in plain English and include one natural keyword (e.g. "a life lesson", "the Bhagavad Gita", "mindset").
   - Good: "Boss shouted at you in front of everyone? Arjuna felt worse, on a battlefield."
   - Bad: "VIRAL!!! You won't BELIEVE this 🤯" (clickbait), or a list of keywords.
2. **Story / context** (1–2 short lines): the scripture moment told simply, with the verse reference where there is one.
3. **Takeaway** (1 line): one meaningful, practical life lesson the viewer can use today.
4. **Engagement prompt** (conversational, 1–2 lines; vary it):
   - a comment question people can answer in a few words, tied to their own life;
   - a share or save nudge that names a person or moment ("Send this to the friend who…", "Save this for your next bad Monday");
   - a follow line, e.g. "Follow @sutraverselife for daily life lessons from ancient wisdom."
5. **Keyword line** (one line, after a blank line), 10–15 keywords in total, separated by " · ", no repeats, written in readable Title Case:
   - **Always** the three brand keywords: SutraVerse · Ancient Wisdom · Life Lessons
   - **4–7 broad discovery keywords**, chosen to fit the topic, from: Instagram Reels · Reels Instagram · Motivational Reels · Inspirational Content · Daily Motivation · Personal Growth · Self Improvement · Mindset · Life Advice · Wisdom · Human Psychology · Positive Thinking · Practical Life Lessons · Trending Reels · Viral Reel. Use at most ONE of "Viral Reel" / "Trending Reels" per caption, and only in this keyword line, never as a claim.
   - **0–4 scripture or theme keywords, only when they genuinely relate to the Reel**: e.g. Bhagavad Gita · Ramayana · Mahabharata · Upanishads · Vedas · Puranas · Chanakya Niti · Sanatan Dharma · Karma · Dharma · Spiritual Wisdom · the main character's name.
   - Pick the set by search intent: what would someone who should see this Reel type into Instagram search?
6. **Exactly 5 hashtags** (one line, after a blank line):
   - #SutraVerse (always first);
   - 1–2 topic-specific tags (the story, character, scripture or theme, e.g. #BhagavadGita, #Karma, #Hanuman);
   - 2–3 broad discovery tags (e.g. #LifeLessons, #AncientWisdom, #SelfImprovement, #Mindset, #MotivationalReels, #DailyMotivation, #PersonalGrowth).
   - No banned, spammy or unrelated tags; never more or fewer than 5.

Length: total under 2,200 characters; aim for about 500–1,100. Short paragraphs, 1–3 tasteful emojis at most. English, with a Hinglish phrase only when it makes a line more relatable.

## Pinned first comment
One easy, specific question that invites a real answer (not "thoughts?"). Different from the caption's question.

## On-screen and in-video SEO
- The cover title and the first on-screen English text state the topic clearly in plain words (e.g. "Krishna's 1 Rule for Overthinking"), so Instagram and viewers understand it.
- Subtitles stay English; key terms (the character, scripture or the life lesson) appear on screen at least once.

## Tone
Engaging, insightful, modern, culturally respectful, accessible to a broad audience (not only devotees). Deities and scriptures are always described with respect; the lesson is framed for everyone's daily life.
