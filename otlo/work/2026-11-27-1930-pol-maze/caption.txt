Lost in an Ahmedabad pol? That's not a mistake. It's the design.

The word pol comes from the Sanskrit pratoli, "gate". Each pol sat behind one big gate: shut it, and outsiders couldn't get in.
Inside, lanes led to more lanes and khadkis, and narrow passages linked house to house, so families could slip out during a raid and pass messages roof to roof.
In 2017, this walled city of pols became India's first World Heritage City.
