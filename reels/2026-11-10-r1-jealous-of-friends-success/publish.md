# Caption

Jealous of a friend's success? Muted his "new car" post instead of saying congrats? 👇

The idea: upward social comparison. Psychologist Leon Festinger (1954, Human Relations) showed we judge ourselves by comparing with others, and it stings most when the other person is like us, just ahead. In the Mahabharata (Sabha Parva 2.43), Duryodhana slips at the Pandavas' Maya Sabha, is laughed at, and tells Shakuni: "My heart burns." The palace was theirs. The fire was his. (The "blind man's son" taunt is a later retelling, not in this scene of the text.)

Verse: Yoga Sutras 1.33. मैत्रीकरुणामुदितोपेक्षाणां सुखदुःखपुण्यापुण्यविषयाणां भावनातश्चित्तप्रसादनम्. Toward the happy, friendliness; toward the suffering, compassion; toward the good, gladness; toward the wicked, indifference: so the mind stays clear.
