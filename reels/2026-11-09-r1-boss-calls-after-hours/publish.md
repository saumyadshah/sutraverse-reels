## Caption
Boss calls after office hours? How to say no to an 8:47 pm "quick call" without souring things 👇

The idea: the positive no. It comes from negotiation expert William Ury in The Power of a Positive No (2007). It has three parts: Yes! → No → Yes?. First, say yes to what you're protecting. Then give a clear, calm no. Then offer a better yes. (Paraphrase)

Your move tomorrow: when the late ping comes, reply in three lines. "Sir, tonight is family dinner. Can't take a call right now. 10 am tomorrow, first thing? Send the agenda, I'll be ready."
