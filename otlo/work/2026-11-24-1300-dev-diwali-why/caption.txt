Diwali was two weeks ago. So why are the diyas coming out again tonight?

Tonight is Dev Diwali, on Kartik Purnima: Tuesday, 24 November 2026 (Purnima tithi till about 8:23 pm, per Drik Panchang).
As the old story goes, Tripurasura's three flying cities could fall only when all three lined up, to a single arrow. On Kartik Purnima they did, and Lord Shiva became Tripurari.
It is believed the gods then came to Kashi, bathed in the Ganga and lit diyas on the ghats. Varanasi's ghats still glow tonight, and many people float lamps on the water.
