A desert made of salt, not sand. And on full-moon nights, it turns silver.

In the monsoon, the Great Rann of Kutch fills with water. Through the dry months it dries out, leaving a white sheet of salt.
Rann Utsav at Dhordo runs 1 November 2026 to 7 March 2027, and this season's first full-moon nights are 23–25 November: right now. Next: December, January and February.
Evenings bring Kutchi folk music and dance under the moon.
