Same undhiyu, two colours: green in Surat, red in Kathiyawad.

First, the name: undhiyu comes from "undhu", upside down. It was traditionally cooked in an earthen matlu, turned upside down in a pit with the fire on top.
Surti undhiyu runs on a green masala (coconut, coriander, green garlic, green chilli), stuffed into brinjal, potato and raw banana.
Kathiyawadi undhiyu goes red: red chilli, more heat, bigger pieces and nothing stuffed.
