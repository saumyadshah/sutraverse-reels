# SutraVerse Reel caption & Instagram SEO strategy

> Also read toolkit/CONTENT_STANDARDS.md (hooks, story structure, shloka explanations, visuals, carousels, Stories). It overrides older instructions where they conflict.

Applies to every SutraVerse Reel AND carousel caption and its metadata (carousel differences in "Carousels" below). Goal: a recognisable SutraVerse identity plus relevant organic discovery, watch time, saves, shares, meaningful comments and follows. Relevance and readability beat volume: no keyword stuffing, no misleading clickbait, no irrelevant trending terms, no promises of virality.

## Caption structure (in this order)

1. **Hook line** (first ~125 characters, before Instagram's "…more"): instant interest from a relatable modern moment or a surprising story beat. Name the topic in plain English and include one natural keyword (e.g. "a life lesson", "the Bhagavad Gita", "mindset").
   - Good: "Boss shouted at you in front of everyone? Arjuna felt worse, on a battlefield."
   - Bad: "VIRAL!!! You won't BELIEVE this 🤯" (clickbait), or a list of keywords.
2. **Story / context** (1–2 short lines): the scripture moment told simply, with the verse reference where there is one.
3. **Takeaway** (1 line): one meaningful, practical life lesson the viewer can use today.
4. **Engagement prompt** (conversational, 1–2 lines; vary it):
   - a comment question people can answer in a few words, tied to their own life;
   - a share or save nudge that names a person or moment ("Send this to the friend who…", "Save this for your next bad Monday");
   - a follow line, e.g. "Follow @sutraverselife for daily life lessons from ancient wisdom."
5. **Keyword line** (one line, after a blank line), 10–15 keywords in total, separated by " · ", no repeats, written in readable Title Case:
   - **Always** the three brand keywords: SutraVerse · Ancient Wisdom · Life Lessons
   - **4–7 broad discovery keywords**, chosen to fit the topic, from: Instagram Reels · Reels Instagram · Motivational Reels · Inspirational Content · Daily Motivation · Personal Growth · Self Improvement · Mindset · Life Advice · Wisdom · Human Psychology · Positive Thinking · Practical Life Lessons · Trending Reels · Viral Reel. Use at most ONE of "Viral Reel" / "Trending Reels" per caption, and only in this keyword line, never as a claim.
   - **0–4 scripture or theme keywords, only when they genuinely relate to the Reel**: e.g. Bhagavad Gita · Ramayana · Mahabharata · Upanishads · Vedas · Puranas · Chanakya Niti · Sanatan Dharma · Karma · Dharma · Spiritual Wisdom · the main character's name.
   - Pick the set by search intent: what would someone who should see this Reel type into Instagram search?
6. **Exactly 5 hashtags** (one line, after a blank line):
   - #SutraVerse (always first);
   - 1–2 topic-specific tags (the story, character, scripture or theme, e.g. #BhagavadGita, #Karma, #Hanuman);
   - 2–3 broad discovery tags (e.g. #LifeLessons, #AncientWisdom, #SelfImprovement, #Mindset, #MotivationalReels, #DailyMotivation, #PersonalGrowth).
   - Choose them from the pools in "Hashtag & keyword research" below. No banned, spammy or unrelated tags; never more or fewer than 5.

Length: total under 2,200 characters; aim for about 500–1,100. Short paragraphs, 1–3 tasteful emojis at most. English, with a Hinglish phrase only when it makes a line more relatable.

## Hashtag & keyword research (applies to every Reel)

Instagram enforces a maximum of 5 hashtags per post/Reel (since Dec 2025), counting caption and first comment together. So hashtags never go in the first comment, and the real discovery levers are the words Instagram indexes: the caption (especially line 1), the on-screen English text, and the spoken audio.

### 1. The search we're trying to win
Before writing anything, write down the ONE phrase a real person would type into Instagram search that this Reel should answer, e.g.:
"bhagavad gita life lessons", "how to stop overthinking", "krishna motivation", "mahabharata story", "karna story", "hanuman story", "chanakya niti", "ancient wisdom for modern life", "life lessons in hindi", "gita quotes for anxiety".
Pick by the Reel's real topic. Prefer a specific problem + source ("gita for overthinking") over a generic one ("motivation").

### 2. Caption keyword (line 1)
The searchable phrase appears naturally in the first line or the first sentence, e.g. "The Bhagavad Gita's fix for overthinking at 2 a.m. 🪔". Never a bare keyword list as the hook.

### 3. Spoken keyword (in the narration)
Within the first 5 seconds of the Hindi voice-over, say the searchable phrase out loud, clearly, in natural Hinglish, e.g. "Bhagavad Gita ka ye ek shloka overthinking ka ilaaj hai…", "Mahabharat ki ye kahani…". Say the character's and scripture's names clearly at least once more later. Transcripts are indexed.

### 4. On-screen keyword
The first on-screen English text and the cover title contain the topic words ("Gita's Fix for Overthinking"), and the subtitle line that carries the spoken keyword shows it in English.

### 5. Hashtags: CORE + ROTATING, exactly 5
- CORE (every Reel): #SutraVerse
- BROAD discovery (pick 2–3 that fit; rotate, don't reuse the same trio every day):
  #LifeLessons #AncientWisdom #SelfImprovement #Mindset #PersonalGrowth #DailyMotivation #Spirituality #MotivationalReels #Wisdom #PositiveThinking
- TOPIC / niche (pick 1–2 that genuinely match):
  - Gita: #BhagavadGita #GitaWisdom #Krishna #KarmaYoga
  - Mahabharata: #Mahabharata #Mahabharat #Karna #Arjuna
  - Ramayana: #Ramayana #Ramayan #Hanuman #JaiShriRam
  - Upanishads / Vedas: #Upanishads #Vedanta #Vedas #VedicWisdom
  - Puranas: #HinduMythology #BhagavataPurana #Shiva #Vishnu
  - Niti / Yoga: #ChanakyaNiti #Chanakya #YogaSutras #Patanjali
  - Broad faith tag only when the Reel is devotional: #SanatanDharma #HinduDharma
  - Festival tags (#Diwali, #Navratri…) only on the festival's Reel.
- Order: #SutraVerse, then topic tag(s), then broad tags.

### 6. AVOID
- Generic, low-intent or engagement-bait tags: #fyp #viral #explorepage #explore #reels #reelsinstagram #trending #instagood #follow4follow #like4like #foryou.
- Off-topic trending tags, tags about other creators/brands, misspelled or banned tags, and political or communal tags.
- More or fewer than 5 hashtags; hashtags in the first comment.

## Pinned first comment
One easy, specific question that invites a real answer (not "thoughts?"). Different from the caption's question.

## On-screen and in-video SEO
- The cover title and the first on-screen English text state the topic clearly in plain words (e.g. "Krishna's 1 Rule for Overthinking"), so Instagram and viewers understand it.
- Subtitles stay English; key terms (the character, scripture or the life lesson) appear on screen at least once.

## Carousels (same strategy, adapted)
Everything above applies to carousel posts too, with these differences:
- No narration, so there is no spoken keyword. Instead the COVER slide title carries the search phrase's topic words in plain English (e.g. "Gita's Fix for Overthinking"), and the topic words appear again on at least one inside slide.
- Caption line 1 contains the search phrase naturally; the takeaway can point to the slides ("Slide 4 is the one to screenshot").
- Engagement prompt leans on SAVE and SHARE (carousels are saved and sent more than Reels), plus one easy comment question and the follow line.
- Same keyword line (always starting "SutraVerse · Ancient Wisdom · Life Lessons") and exactly 5 hashtags from the same pools; no hashtags in the first comment.
- Add alt text per slide when the tool allows it (plain description + the topic words).

## Tone
Engaging, insightful, modern, culturally respectful, accessible to a broad audience (not only devotees). Deities and scriptures are always described with respect; the lesson is framed for everyone's daily life.
