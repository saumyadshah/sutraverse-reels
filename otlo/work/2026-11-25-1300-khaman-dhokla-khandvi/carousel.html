Spot the difference
Three Gujarati farsan people mix up
સ્વાઇપ કરો · Swipe →
01
ખમણ
Khaman: besan, quick-risen
Made from besan (gram flour) or soaked chana dal. Often made instant: soda or fruit salt makes the batter rise, so it steams up bright yellow, soft and spongy. A sweet-and-sour tempering goes on top.
ચણાનો લોટ · પીળું · પોચું ને જાળીદાર
02
ઢોકળાં
Dhokla: rice + dal, fermented
Made from a batter of rice and dal (lentils), soaked, ground and left to ferment overnight. It comes out paler, a little firmer than khaman, with a slight tang.
ચોખા + દાળ · આથો · આછું ને ખાટું
03
ખાંડવી
Khandvi: cooked, spread, rolled
Besan whisked with buttermilk or yogurt, cooked down to a thick paste, spread thin while hot, then cut into strips and rolled up into tight, bite-sized rolls.
ચણાનો લોટ + છાશ · પાતળું પાથરો · વીંટો
