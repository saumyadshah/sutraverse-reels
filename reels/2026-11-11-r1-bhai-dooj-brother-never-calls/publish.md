## Caption
Bhai dooj pe sister ko "seen" pe chhod diya? Brother never calls because it feels awkward? 👇

The idea: the reaching-out gap. Liu, Rim, Min & Min (2022, Journal of Personality and Social Psychology; 13 experiments, 5,900+ people) found that people who send a "just because" message to someone they've lost touch with consistently underestimate how much it is appreciated. The receiver's big feeling is surprise: "Arre, usne yaad kiya!" (The studies were about reconnecting after a gap, not after a fight.)

Verse: Atharvaveda 3.30.3. मा भ्राता भ्रातरं द्विक्षन्मा स्वसारमुत स्वसा । सम्यञ्चः सव्रता भूत्वा वाचं वदत भद्रया ॥ "Let not brother hate brother, nor sister sister. United, of one purpose, speak your words kindly." (after Whitney 1905 & Griffith 1895). Tradition holds that on Bhai Dooj, Yama visited his sister Yamuna. Yamraj ko bhi time mil gaya tha.
