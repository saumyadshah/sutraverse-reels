Why does Gujarat's wedding season wait for a plant's wedding?

Today, Kartak Sud Baras (Sat 21 Nov), Tulsi weds Bhagwan Vishnu in his Shaligram form.
Yesterday was Dev Uthi Agiyaras. It's believed Vishnu wakes after the four months of Chaturmas, and with this vivah the wedding muhurats return.
In homes without a daughter, many families perform Tulsi's kanyadaan themselves.

આજે કારતક સુદ બારસ: તુલસી અને શાલિગ્રામનાં લગ્ન, અને લગ્નની સિઝન ફરી શરૂ.
