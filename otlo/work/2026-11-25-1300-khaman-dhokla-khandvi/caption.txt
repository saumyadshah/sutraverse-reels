Khaman and dhokla are the same thing? Not quite. And khandvi is different again.

Khaman is besan or chana dal, often made instant with soda or fruit salt, so it steams up bright yellow and spongy.
Dhokla starts from a rice-and-dal batter fermented overnight: paler, a little firmer, slightly tangy. Khandvi is besan cooked with buttermilk, spread thin and rolled.
