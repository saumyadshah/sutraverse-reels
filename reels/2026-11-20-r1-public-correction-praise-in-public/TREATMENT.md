# TREATMENT — Office Shastra EP 04 · "Galti meeting mein ya akele?"
Series: Office Shastra · Format: Versus · Treatment: Night City Cel (base: midcentury-toon acting economy + dark-keynote UI cards) · Verse: Bhagavad Gita 17.15 · Experiment: COVER TEST (numbered-course card header on frame 0)

## Structures considered
1. **Versus (chosen):** the same mistake corrected twice — Malhotra's way (public, about the person) then Riya's way (private, about the work) — split card, then the verse. The format is the lesson: viewers see both and pick.
2. Countdown "3 lines that fix a mistake without wounding" — strong save value but loses the face-on-the-call hook.
3. Before/after on Sana alone (her face in both rooms) — emotional, but the manager (who sends this) has no role.
Chosen 1: dialogue-first unfair line on a face (our best hook type), a two-person micro-conflict every employee has lived, and a copyable sentence at the end.

## Logline / arc
A wrong number on slide 7 gets Sana scolded in front of the client; ten minutes later her new lead Riya fixes the same mistake in a 1:1 without the wound. Setup (the call) → turn (why public correction backfires: Edmondson) → ending (Riya's 3-part sentence, Gita 17.15, "praise in public, correct in private", either/or).

## Benchmark
Learn (our EP 02 / RULE 02, @balakheji): open on the other person's unfair line on a face; the exact better sentence at the end; one authority. Don't learn: long held UI cards, villain caricature (Malhotra is stressed, not evil), source lists on screen.
