# TREATMENT — 2026-11-08-r2 · Office Shastra EP 01 · Night City Cel (base: midcentury-toon + dark-keynote)

**Candidate structures.** (1) Story → Framework: the theft, two bad options, a freeze, Option C in three steps. (2) Before/after: the same CXO meeting played twice, once with no record and once with the email. (3) Countdown to 10 a.m.: the night before, ticking. **Chosen: 1.** It is the series format, it fits the pilot, and it gives Chanakya's verse a crossover moment between the problem and the method. The opening image is the boss's unfair line landing on Riya's face. The ending is a question, not a lecture.

**Logline.** Riya's VP presents her deck as "team" work. Chanakya Niti 2.7 says to guard a plan like secret counsel and then put it to work, so the better move is to put her name and a timestamp on the work before the meeting.
**Arc.** Setup (the theft, the CXO review) → turn (two bad options, the 2,300-year-old third road) → ending (Option C in three steps, the principle card, either/or).

**Benchmark.** Learn: the hook-mining result (the other person's unfair line on the hero's face, 28–36% skip). Don't learn: narrator-first openings or "Did you know".
