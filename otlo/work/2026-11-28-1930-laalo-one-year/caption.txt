One year ago this week, a ₹50 lakh Gujarati film did what no Gujarati film had done before.

Laalo – Krishna Sada Sahaayate had no big stars, a first-time director (Ankit Sakhiya) and almost no takers on release. Then the tide turned: ₹100 crore worldwide in 51 days, breaking Chaal Jeevi Laiye's record from 2019.
