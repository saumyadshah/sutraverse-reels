Got a ₹100 note in your wallet? Turn it over 👀
તમારી ₹100ની નોટ પાછળ જે છે, એ સદીઓ સુધી જમીનમાં દટાયેલું હતું.

That's Rani ki Vav in Patan. When the RBI launched the lavender ₹100 note in July 2018, it chose this stepwell for the back.
A queen, Udayamati, built it in the 11th century in memory of her husband, King Bhima I, as an upside-down temple going seven storeys down.
Then the Saraswati flooded and silt swallowed it whole. That same silt protected the carvings: when British archaeologists Henry Cousens and James Burgess visited in the 1890s, it was completely buried. Excavation began in the 1940s, the ASI restored it in the 1980s, and UNESCO listed it in 2014.

Patan is about 134 km from Ahmedabad (Gujarat Tourism), an easy day trip 🚗
