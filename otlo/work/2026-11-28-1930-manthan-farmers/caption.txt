Manthan (1976), directed by Shyam Benegal, tells the story of Gujarat's milk cooperative movement, inspired by Dr Verghese Kurien. The dairy farmers of the Gujarat Co-operative Milk Marketing Federation funded it at ₹2 each, roughly the film's ₹10 lakh budget, and its opening titles read "500,000 farmers of Gujarat present".
The cast: Smita Patil, Girish Karnad, Naseeruddin Shah, Amrish Puri. When it released, farmers came by the truckload to watch "their" film.
In 2024, @filmheritagefoundation restored it in 4K (funded by the Gujarat Co-operative Milk Marketing Federation, Amul), and it had its world premiere in Cannes Classics.
